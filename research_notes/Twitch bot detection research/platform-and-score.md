# Twitch platform semantics and Headcount scoring audit

Research date: 2026-10-19. Source pages were retrieved during this research. Most Twitch developer/help pages are living documents without a reliable publication date; retrieval verifies their current wording, not the date each rule first applied. Dated blog posts below predate the requested cutoff. Search-relative ages were not used as publication dates. Some help pages returned a JavaScript shell when opened; claims from those pages are limited to their official indexed text and marked accordingly. Code findings are static inspection of the current checkout, not evidence of production incidents. No application code was changed.

## What can the platform data actually tell us?

### Takeaway
Headcount can identify anomalous public counts and public message-author behavior. It cannot directly count fake video sessions, identify their network origin, or establish who ordered them. The distinction between video watchers, connected chat accounts, and people actually sending messages is essential.

### Cited Findings
- Twitch defines concurrent viewers as people watching live video, including signed-out users. Connected chat accounts are a separate population; chat-only bots need not watch video. Muted and background playback can still count. Count changes can take a few minutes to appear. [Twitch viewer-count explanation](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/understanding-viewer-count-vs-users-in-chat?language=en_US)
- Headcount's `unique_chatters` counts distinct `PRIVMSG` authors in a minute, not everyone connected to chat. Account-age and shared-account signals consequently describe message authors only. [Collector, lines 296–339](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/chat/collector.go:296)
- API capabilities, stated concisely: `Get Chatters` requires moderator authorization; follower totals can be returned without the authorized follower list. `Get Users` supplies creation times, not a suspension-reason field. Public `Get Chat Settings` and `Get Shared Chat Session` accept app tokens. Ad schedules require broadcaster authorization. [Twitch API reference](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api/reference/)
- The current follower collector fetches only a total. Differences are **net follower-count changes**, not observed follow events, follow timestamps, or a list of new followers. `FollowDeltas` distributes those changes evenly across sample gaps. [Helix client](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/helix/client.go:333), [FollowDeltas](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:319)
- Twitch explicitly recognizes malicious botting aimed at other broadcasters, as well as self-promotion through bots. Therefore suspicious activity does not establish broadcaster responsibility. [Twitch, June 17, 2016](https://blog.twitch.tv/en/2016/06/17/on-artificial-viewers-followers-and-chat-activity-317906f1c7ea/)
- Twitch says batches of fake followers can be removed, causing follower dips. This supports follower drops as a possible cleanup signature, not a unique explanation of every decline. Official indexed help text; full article opening returned a shell. [Handling fake engagement](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/how-to-handle-view-follow-bots?language=en_US&sfdcIFrameOrigin=null)

### Inferences
- Label the output an anomaly/suspicion index. A score of 80 is not an 80% probability of botting, an 80% bot share, or evidence the streamer purchased bots. The weights are explicitly hand-set. [Weights and Combine](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:479)
- Retain separate viewer, follow, and chat assessments. A follow-bot attack can be real without fake viewers; legitimate utility bots can produce chat without viewer inflation. A combined score currently loses that distinction. [Component assembly](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/score/score.go:231)
- Do not infer geography, datacenter/residential proxy use, vendor, operator, or compromised-account provenance from these public series. The collector records counts and chatter account IDs, not video-session IP/device/network records. [Collected series](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/score/score.go:170)

### Gaps
- No official exact 3–5-minute Helix refresh guarantee was found. The scorer states that cadence as fact at line 396, whereas official help supports only a general delay. Treat the tolerance as a model choice and estimate sensitivity from observations. [Scorer](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/score/score.go:396), [Twitch count timing](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/understanding-viewer-count-vs-users-in-chat?language=en_US)
- No public field verified here distinguishes suspended, deleted, temporarily omitted, or otherwise unavailable user IDs. The code comment is stronger than the documented API contract. [Users comment](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/helix/client.go:370), [Get Users](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api/reference/#get-users)
- None of these sources establishes the bot share of Twitch or of Headcount's top-200-derived sample.

## Which legitimate mechanisms can resemble the current bot signatures?

### Takeaway
Audience-source changes and chat restrictions can jointly trigger several detectors. Record these as explanatory context before raising weights; many are already available with the existing app-token model.

### Cited Findings
- Embedding is an official Twitch feature. Video-only embeds support autoplay subject to visibility, size, browser, and mobile restrictions. [Embedding documentation](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/embed/video-and-clips/)
- Twitch's front page includes a curated carousel and recommendation shelves. Front-page promotions vary by region and viewer. Official indexed text; full promotion article returned a shell. [Front page](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/what-is-the-front-page?language=en_US), [Promotion FAQ](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/partner-front-page?language=en_US)
- Drops can reward watching for a required duration; watching multiple channels does not accelerate the reward. This creates a non-chat reason to watch. Official indexed text; full article returned a shell. [How Drops work](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/mission-based-drops?language=en_US&sfdcIFrameOrigin=null)
- Shared Chat copies messages across participating rooms and exposes their source-room IDs. Headcount already rejects copied messages whose source room differs from the receiving room. This is a useful existing safeguard. [Twitch IRC Shared Chat](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/chat/irc/#shared-chat), [Collector filter](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/chat/collector.go:302)
- Twitch announced combined unique concurrent viewership for approved co-stream events and discovery improvements on October 14, 2025. This is a reason to distinguish discovery/display aggregates from individual stream counts. Do not assume every visible Twitch count has the same denominator. [Co-streaming announcement](https://blog.twitch.tv/en/2025/10/14/co-streaming-on-twitch/)
- EventSub `channel.raid` requires no broadcaster authorization, but its documentation says it only notifies for raids that appear in chat. It can improve transport resilience without becoming proof every audience transfer is observed. [EventSub channel.raid](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/eventsub/eventsub-subscription-types/#channelraid)
- The current collector records follower-only, slow, subscriber-only and emote-only room state, but score construction marks chat restricted only for subscriber/emote modes occupying more than 30% of the stream. Only `chat_engagement` gets that restriction discount. [Room state](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/chat/collector.go:393), [Build](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/score/score.go:203), [Discount](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:456)

### Inferences
- Legitimate video-only traffic can lift viewers without comparable chat or follow growth. Such traffic could trigger `unexplained_jumps`, `spike_chat_lift`, `spike_follow_lift`, and `chat_engagement` together. This is a mechanism-based confounder, not a claim all embeds are legitimate or all are counted identically. [Detector inputs](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:132)
- Followers-only gates can directly suppress new-arrival chat, exactly when `spike_chat_lift` expects newcomers to speak. Replace the whole-stream binary flag with minute/episode-level settings and gate response measurements by the settings during that episode. Use slow-mode duration, emote-only and subscriber-only status as separate covariates. [Spike episodes](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:237)
- Shared Chat participants, raids, common events, and communities can create legitimate cross-channel synchrony and account overlap. Exclude or model known collaboration/raid edges in `shared_accounts` and `synchronized_spikes`; young account age alone does not establish that channels are unrelated. [Shared query](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/store/shared.go:21), [Synchrony](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/sync.go:55)
- Poll public chat settings and Shared Chat sessions on a modest cadence and on relevant IRC notices. Existing source-room filtering is not a substitute for recording collaboration membership. Public raid EventSub webhooks are feasible with subscription lifecycle/signature handling; chat EventSub is a separate authorization/scale decision. [API reference](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api/reference/), [Chat authentication](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/chat/authenticating/)
- Ad breaks are plausible explanations to investigate for count/engagement changes, but the research did not verify a universal numerical effect on Helix counts. Broadcaster-authorized ads data or independently supplied annotations can support evaluation; public count shape alone cannot identify an ad break. [Ad authorization](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api/reference/#get-ad-schedule)

### Gaps
- Current public endpoints reviewed here do not provide a complete, verified referral-source or embed-traffic breakdown for arbitrary channels. Do not promise automatic embed/front-page attribution.
- Exact Helix `viewer_count` behavior during every form of Shared Viewership/co-stream aggregate was not established. Verify a known live collaboration and preserve response payloads before designing a shared-viewer adjustment.
- No universal chat-per-viewer, follow-conversion, or chat-lift threshold was justified by official Twitch documentation.

## What should change in the calculation and measurement pipeline?

### Takeaway
Prioritize measurement validity, dependency control, and validation over new heuristic weights. Several exact code behaviors can inflate or overstate evidence even before considering adaptive bots.

### Cited Findings
1. **Account lookup state is stale by design. Verified static behavior.** `lookupUsers` persists an omitted ID as a nil-created account; `InsertUsers` turns that into `missing=true`; `UnknownUsers` only returns IDs with no database row. Consequently a retained missing record is not retried, and a retained found record is not checked for later disappearance. API request errors are correctly retried; the issue concerns successful responses omitting an ID. [Lookup](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/chat/collector.go:538), [Missing persistence](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/store/chat.go:143), [Selection](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/store/chat.go:108)
2. **Lookup coverage is hidden. Verified static behavior.** `ChatterAccounts` inner-joins cached users; unresolved/pending user IDs disappear from both total and numerator. It can report an apparently complete age share for only the subset successfully cached. [Account query](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/store/score.go:162)
3. **Family caps do not enforce causal independence. Verified arithmetic from code.** A maximal `periodic_spikes` contributes .6 and maximal `spike_chat_lift` .5; viewer-shape cap is `.3+.5=.8` and spike-response cap is `.3+.6=.9`; both survive their caps and produce `100*(1-(1-.6)*(1-.5))=80`. Yet the second detector depends on the same first-stage viewer spike. Likewise global quiet-chat and low chat-lift can be explained by a single audience-source shift. The grouping is useful, but two family labels do not guarantee two independent causes. [Families and Combine](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:498)
4. **Confidence is availability, not correctness probability. Verified formula.** It is available detector weight divided by total weight, multiplied by observed span/180 minutes capped at one. Several zero-spike detectors remain available even with no discriminating events. The span is `len(d.Viewers)`, not effective independent viewer refreshes or fully healthy collection minutes. [Combine](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:518), [Build](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/score/score.go:170)
5. **Preprocessing treats identical readings as unavailable. Verified static behavior; distortion risk requires evaluation.** Every repeated value becomes NaN and is interpolated between distinct values. Identical counts can also be genuinely stable; the data contain no cache timestamp proving otherwise. Interpolation can move apparent changes earlier and bridge missing collection periods. [HeldToNaN and Interpolate](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/series.go:15)
6. **IRC ingestion lacks visible message-ID deduplication. Verified handler behavior; real incident not established.** `onPrivmsg` increments before any message-ID dedupe. Twitch documents that IRC messages can arrive repeatedly and out of order. Duplicate delivery could affect author activity and repetition counts. [Handler](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/chat/collector.go:296), [IRC parsing contract](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/chat/irc/#parsing-messages)
7. **Follower burst event counts depend on sampling gaps. Verified mechanism.** `FollowDeltas` spreads a net change across every minute of a gap; `FollowAnomalies` counts each qualifying interpolated minute as a burst/drop. Report affected duration separately from independently observed change events. Hot/tracked/live-cold follower sampling is 1/2/10 minutes. [Deltas and anomalies](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:319), [Sampling](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/tracker/poller.go:239)
8. **Training/scoring definitions differ. Verified static behavior; practical size unmeasured.** Peer stats use raw joined viewer samples at viewers>=100; scoring uses interpolated viewers and accepts viewers>=50. Peers include the scored population and require only 30 joined samples. Baseline fitting trims outliers, but does not exclude the target channel or balance streams per channel. [Peers](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/store/score.go:192), [Scoring metric](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:433), [Fit](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/score/baseline.go:65)
9. **Current anonymous IRC differs from documented authentication. Verified documentation/code mismatch; not proof collector is failing.** Code uses a `justinfan` account and `oauth:anonymous`; current IRC documentation describes a user token with read/edit scopes and recommends EventSub. Live support for anonymous collection should be measured, and outages must remain missing data. Existing ROOMSTATE/connection-health checks for quiet zero rows are useful. [Connection](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/chat/collector.go:151), [Health-aware flush](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/chat/collector.go:440), [IRC docs](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/chat/irc/)
10. **Observation bias and cold-tier blind spots. Verified architecture.** Sampling is seeded by historical global top-200 entry; cold channels lose chat collection and move to sparse viewer sampling. Findings cannot be generalized to all Twitch. Novel chat-only abuse in a cold channel may not trigger reconsideration until some other signal changes. [README](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/README.md:1), [Chat joins](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/chat/collector.go:206)

### Inferences
- **First implementation slice:** replace `missing` with an explicit lookup-status model; keep creation-date facts separate from current availability, record last checked time, retry omissions with backoff, and periodically recheck a bounded sample. Expose resolved/all-observed-author coverage. Do not give one omission suspension-level evidentiary weight. This needs schema/collector/scorer work but no new Twitch permission. [Current persistence](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/store/chat.go:134)
- **Second slice:** add source message-ID dedupe in a bounded in-memory cache, preserve source timestamps for ordering, and track message/room/connection collection quality separately. Test duplicates, reconnections, partial minutes, and delayed arrivals. [Current collector](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/chat/collector.go:296)
- **Third slice:** make viewer-shape and spike-response features one causal group until data show their incremental discrimination. Prefer per-abuse-type scores and an evidence graph describing shared inputs. Evaluate a conservative maximum within highly correlated signals or a learned model with interaction terms; do not present a new combination formula as calibrated without labels. [Current combination](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:518)
- **Fourth slice:** preserve raw counts, poll timestamps, missingness, and effective resolution. Compare held/staircase/linear-interpolation variants; do not bridge long gaps. Test timing uncertainty by shifting onsets across a plausible lag range and show whether conclusions survive. Gate synchrony on empirical count resolution and use block/circular-shift nulls that preserve each channel's autocorrelation and shared time-of-day effects. The current binomial test corrects partner multiplicity but does not model those dependencies or repeated rescoring. [Preprocessing](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/series.go:15), [Synchrony](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/sync.go:55)
- **Validation design:** split by channel and forward time so repeat streams/accounts do not leak into both sides; hold out event/Drops/front-page/Shared Chat periods; use target-excluded peer fits with equalized channel influence; report precision-recall and false-positive rate by language/category/size/data coverage. Labeled incidents and owner-authorized traffic-source data are needed. Synthetic tests establish intended formula behavior, not real-world detection accuracy. [Current synthetic tests](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors_test.go:11)
- **User-facing confidence:** separate data coverage, effective independent observations, model validation status, and strength of evidence. A highly unusual stream can be well observed but still have an uncertain explanation. Keep score and known confounders visible independently. [Current confidence](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/detect/detectors.go:518)
- **Measure evasion explicitly:** smooth constant fake viewers, older/compromised accounts, human-like generated chat, channel-specific bots, and campaigns targeting untracked channels may evade current spike/new-account/shared-author rules. These are expected blind spots inferred from feature design, not measured prevalence claims. [Current detector set](/Users/onnwee/Work/Subcult/sus-quotient/internal/score/score.go:231)

### Gaps
- No production logs, database extracts, or replay were used. These findings establish code behavior and testable failure hypotheses, not the frequency or actual impact of failures.
- No defensible new numerical weights, age thresholds, probability calibration, or bot percentage can be supplied from these sources alone.
- The baseline contamination and interpolation effects need replay/ablation to quantify. Treat them as evaluation priorities, not proof that current rankings are wrong.
